THE PLAYBOOK

Know it. Lock it. Clash.

  1. Choose Solo Sprint for a category, or Clash Mode for a mixed challenge.
  2. Pick Easy (20s), Medium (15s), or Hard (12s). Answer before time runs out.
  3. Correct answers earn 100 base points, up to 100 speed points, and up to 100 streak points.
  4. In Clash Mode, your multiplier rises every three consecutive correct answers, up to 4×. Every three questions, the clock gets two seconds shorter (minimum 6s).
  5. A wrong answer or timeout resets your streak. Your final rank reflects accuracy.

Use 1–4 to answer and Enter to continue, or tap any answer. Leaderboards are stored on your device.
